The Origin Tale

FROM A BASEMENT D&D CAMPAIGN TO A TABLETOP HAVEN

In the winter of 2018, four lifelong friends realized that modern life was missing something vital: genuine, face-to-face connection across a wooden table over dice and strategy.

What began as an underground library of hand-sleeved board games in Seattle has evolved into Hobbix: a premier 4,500 sq. ft. community gaming lounge, coffee tavern, and worldwide distributor of the finest board games and hobby gear on Earth.

Our Core Values

Our Tabletop Philosophy

Four guiding principles that shape how we curate our collection and host our community.

01

Unplugged Face-to-Face Joy

In an era dominated by screens and algorithmic feeds, board games provide tactile, deeply human moments of shared laughter, triumphant tactical maneuvers, and unforgettable table memories.

02

Zero Gatekeeping, All Welcome

Whether you've never rolled a 20-sided die or you own 400 heavy Euro games, there is always an open chair, warm coffee, and an enthusiastic teacher ready at Hobbix.

03

Eco-Conscious Tabletop

We champion publishers who use FSC-certified card stock, plant-based inks, and plastic-free interior packaging to protect our planet for future generations of gamers.

04

Artistic Craftsmanship

From hand-cast resin dice to linen-finish cards and sculpted miniatures, we celebrate tabletop gaming as a profound visual and tactile artistic medium.
