Tabletop Masterclass Bites

Quick advice from our tournament judges and miniature artists.

Sleeving Thick Micron Rule

Use 100-micron matte sleeves for deckbuilders with heavy shuffling, and 60-micron standard sleeves for auxiliary market decks to save shelf space.

The 2 Thin Coats Mantra

Never apply paint straight from the pot to miniature plastic. Thin with water on a wet palette to preserve crisp sculpted armor details.

How to Teach Heavy Games

Always start with the winning condition (How to score points), explain the thematic role of players, and run 1 dummy round before diving into edge cases.
